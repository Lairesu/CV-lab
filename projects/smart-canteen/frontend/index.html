    How does it work?

    Each table runs in one of two modes. In manual mode, staff set the status. In automatic mode, YOLO looks for a person inside the table's zone on a recorded video: a person seen in the last two minutes shows Occupied (a camera estimate), otherwise Unknown. The camera never marks a table Available, because an empty-looking seat may still be taken.

    ◉ Behind the scenes: camera analysis

    Saved observations from the recorded-video experiment. A detected person does not always mean a table is taken, and no detection does not prove it is free.
